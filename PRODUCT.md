# Rutinko Product Context

Rutinko is a Croatian mobile-first PWA for daily routines, simple obligations, reminders, and mini exercise habits.

## Product promise

Rutinko is a daily autopilot for things the user does not want to keep in their head.

The app should help users remember and complete basic daily essentials:

- brush teeth
- eat breakfast
- take medication
- drink water
- do mini exercise, such as 20 sit-ups in the morning and evening
- shower
- pay bills
- file taxes
- take the car to service
- call someone important

## Core user need

The user does not want a complex todo app, calendar, habit tracker, or productivity system.

They want a simple loop:

1. Rutinko reminds them.
2. They tap Done, Snooze 30 min, or Remind in 5 min.
3. If they do nothing, Rutinko keeps reminding them.

## Product lane

Consumer mobile utility with habit and fitness-app polish.

It should feel like a serious everyday product, not a childish checklist.

## Audience

Croatian-speaking users who want less mental clutter and a very simple daily reminder system.

## UX principles

- Croatian UI.
- Mobile-first.
- One-handed use.
- Large tap targets.
- No complex calendar.
- No login in the MVP.
- No backend in the MVP.
- No subscriptions in the MVP.
- No gamification overload.
- The Today screen is the core product.
